Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Fitting for a network built on discretion

This website collects nothing. No personal information, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers. For a network whose first rule is confidentiality, that is the only acceptable starting position.

The nomination form

The request form does not transmit anything. Whatever you write — including names and affiliations — stays in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page. Before correspondence opens, this policy will be rewritten to describe exactly how nomination information is handled, protected, and retained.

Third parties

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google's servers. Nothing else on this site comes from a third party.

Changes

Any change will be posted here, with a new date, before it takes effect.
